CURRENT MAIN ANALYSIS DEFINITION

Candidate T-lineage cells were identified using the stored clustering and reviewed
broad lineage marker profiles. Within each sample, cells at or above the 85th
percentile of the RNA-derived inferCNV score were retained as the CNV-high core
for the main paired pseudobulk analysis. This relative selection retained 1,926
cells across six patient samples, with at least 107 cells in each sample.

As a descriptive reference comparison, the empirical 95th percentile of the
score distribution among 867 putative non-malignant non-T cells was 0.0269455.
A total of 1,978 of 12,828 T-lineage candidates (15.419%) exceeded this fixed score.
Of the 1,926 cells selected by the within-sample top15 rule, 1,407 (73.1%) also
exceeded the reference score. The two rules are therefore not interchangeable.

The within-sample top15 setting was fixed after sensitivity analyses and is the
designated main analysis of this report. It was not prospectively specified.
The reference comparison supports reporting the scale of the high-CNV fraction,
but does not uniquely determine the within-sample percentile or establish its
malignant-cell specificity. Sensitivity results for alternative selection rules
are retained separately. Whole-collection pathway BH adjustment does not account
for the prior selection among analysis specifications.

PATHWAY REPORTING

For both datasets, significant pathways require BH FDR < 0.05 across the full
tested KEGG collection. The cross-dataset intersection additionally requires that
the pathway was tested in both datasets and has matching NES signs. The current
intersection contains 40 shared up-enriched pathways and no shared down-enriched
pathways. Changing this reporting threshold does not change the fitted gene
statistics, nominal pathway P values or the already computed BH FDR values.
